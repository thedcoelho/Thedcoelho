## Hi there 👋
I'm Danillo Leite

Ph.D. candidate in Plant Pathology at Southern Illinois University Carbondale.
I work on soybean stem and seed diseases, and I build tools that make field
research data usable.

**Background:** 7+ years in field-based crop protection research and seed
operations, including three years at Corteva Agriscience in Brazil and the U.S.
I run multi-location field trials, seed health diagnostics, and molecular
pathogen detection.

### What's here

- **[Illinois Soybean Stem Disease Map](https://github.com/thedcoelho/Illinois-Soybean-Stem-Disease-Map)**
  — R Shiny app mapping fungal pathogen prevalence across 45 Illinois counties,
  with a climate correlation module.
  [Live app](https://dcoelho.shinyapps.io/Illinois_Soybean_STEM_Disease_MAP/)

- **[Field Sprayer Calculator](https://github.com/thedcoelho/field-sprayer-calc)**
  — R Shiny tool for sprayer calibration and tank mixing: boom width, GPA,
  travel speed, multi-product mixes.

- **[Soybean Stem Disease Favorability](https://github.com/thedcoelho/Soybean_STEM_Disease_Illinois)**
  — Interactive 3D visualization of environmental and phenological favorability
  spaces for soybean stem diseases.

### Working with

`R` · `Shiny` · `leaflet` · `sf` · `ggplot2` · `Python` · `JMP` · `ARM Research`

Currently building a Python image-analysis tool for seed health and germination
assessment in waterhemp.
